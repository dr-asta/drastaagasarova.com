Meet your doctor

Dr Asta Agasarova

Dr. Asta Agasarova is a highly qualified medical professional with dual medical degrees from prestigious institutions in Russia and Italy. With a background in neurology, she has also gained valuable experience working in the Endocrinology and Metabolic Diseases Departments at the Mater Hospital in Dublin.

Since 2020, Dr. Asta has dedicated her career exclusively to aesthetic and anti-ageing medicine, where her true passion lies. She completed a Level 7 Diploma in Aesthetic Medicine and provides the following treatments: basic and advanced anti-wrinkle injections, lip filler and perioral rejuvenation, dermal fillers, skin boosters, Profhilo, polynucleotides, bio stimulator Sculptra, SkinPen microneedling and peels.

Her approach to aesthetic medicine is grounded in the belief that ‘less is more’. She aims to enhance the natural beauty of her patients, utilising techniques and treatments that deliver subtle yet impactful results.
